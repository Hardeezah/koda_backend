========================================================================
SECTION 1: FORM M - IMPORT DECLARATION
========================================================================

1.1 WHAT IS FORM M?

Form M is a mandatory electronic import declaration document required
by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and the Federal Ministry of
Finance for ALL commercial imports into Nigeria exceeding USD 1,000.
Without a valid Form M, goods CANNOT clear Nigerian customs. There
are no exceptions.

Form M must be registered with an Authorized Dealer Bank (ADB) BEFORE
placing any import order. Processing is done through the CBN Trade
Portal integrated with the B'Odogwu Customs portal.

1.2 REQUIREMENTS TO OPEN FORM M

a) Tax Identification Number (TIN)
   - Must be registered and validated with Nigeria Customs Service
   - Obtained through FIRS (Federal Inland Revenue Service)
   - Required for all trade documentation

b) Proforma Invoice (PFI)
   - Must show: complete description of goods, HS Code, unit price,
     total cost, country of origin, delivery terms
   - Valid for 3 months from date of issuance
   - Must be issued by the foreign supplier/exporter

c) Insurance Certificate
   - Must be issued by a Nigerian-registered insurance company
   - Must cover 110% of the Proforma Invoice value
   - Marine/institute cargo clauses (A) for sea freight

d) Regulatory Permit
   - SONCAP Product Certificate (PC) for applicable products
   - NAFDAC registration/permit for regulated products (drugs, food,
     cosmetics, chemicals, medical devices)
   - Other sector-specific permits as required

e) Completed e-Form M Data Form
   - Processed through the B'Odogwu portal
   - Bank validates all supporting documents

1.3 FORM M VALIDITY PERIODS

Goods Category          Initial Validity    Bank Extension
General Merchandise     180 days            Up to 180 days
Plant & Machinery       365 days            Up to 365 days
Special Commodities     180 days            Case-by-case basis

Further extensions beyond the bank's authority require CBN approval.
Applications for extension must be made before the current validity
expires.

1.4 FORM M PREFIX CODES

BA - Valid for FX
    Importer intends to purchase foreign exchange through official
    CBN channels (NFEM window). Subject to FX availability.

CB - Not Valid for FX
    Importer using own funds, offshore accounts, or other non-CBN
    FX sources. Still requires Form M documentation but does not
    compete for CBN FX allocation.

MF - Another variant for specific transaction types

1.5 FORM M PROCESSING STEPS

Step 1: Importer approaches Authorized Dealer Bank with all documents
Step 2: Bank validates documents and enters data on B'Odogwu portal
Step 3: Form M is assigned a unique reference number
Step 4: Regulatory permits (SONCAP, NAFDAC) are verified
Step 5: Form M is approved and becomes active
Step 6: Importer can proceed with order placement and shipping

1.6 FORM M FEES

- Form M processing: Varies by bank (typically 0.5-1% of value)
- Commission charges: As agreed with the bank
- No fixed CBN fee for Form M itself

1.7 COMMON FORM M REJECTED REASONS

- Expired Proforma Invoice (PFI older than 3 months)
- Missing or invalid SONCAP/NAFDAC permits
- HS code mismatch between PFI and permit
- Insurance certificate not from Nigerian insurer
- TIN not validated with Customs
- Incomplete product description

SECTION 2: FORM NXP - EXPORT DECLARATION
========================================================================

2.1 WHAT IS FORM NXP?

The Nigerian Export Proceeds (NXP) form is a mandatory document for
ALL commercial export transactions from Nigeria. It links the export
shipment to expected foreign currency inflow. Without a valid NXP
form, cargo CANNOT legally leave Nigeria.

2.2 REQUIREMENTS TO PROCESS FORM NXP

a) Domiciliary Account
   - Must be with a CBN-licensed Authorized Dealer Bank
   - Account denominated in USD, GBP, EUR, or other approved currency

b) NEPC Registration
   - Nigerian Export Promotion Council registration certificate
   - Required for all exporters

c) CAC Registration
   - Corporate Affairs Commission certificate of incorporation
   - Must be current and valid

d) Proforma Invoice
   - From the foreign buyer/importer
   - Showing product description, quantity, unit price, total value,
     delivery terms, payment terms

e) Product-Specific Certificates
   - Phytosanitary certificate (for agricultural products)
   - SON conformity certificate (SONCAP)
   - NAFDAC registration (for regulated products)
   - Certificate of Origin (general or AfCFTA)

2.3 FORM NXP FEES AND PROCESSING

2026 Reform: Form NXP processing is now FREE of charge.
Processing fees were removed under the Fourth Edition FX Manual
effective June 1, 2026.

2.4 EXPORT PROCEEDS REPATRIATION

- Export proceeds must be repatriated within 90 DAYS of shipment
  for non-oil exports
- Failure triggers: NXP flagging, account restrictions, penalties
- 1% penalty for non-compliance with repatriation deadline
- Oil and gas exports have separate repatriation frameworks

2.5 FORM NXP PROCESSING STEPS

Step 1: Exporter approaches bank with all documents
Step 2: Bank validates and enters data on B'Odogwu portal
Step 3: NXP is assigned a reference number
Step 4: Customs verification and pre-export inspection
Step 5: NXP is approved
Step 6: Shipment can proceed
Step 7: Proceeds must be received and confirmed within 90 days

SECTION 3: CBN FOREIGN EXCHANGE FRAMEWORK 2026
========================================================================

3.1 KEY CHANGES IN FOURTH EDITION FX MANUAL (Effective June 1, 2026)

Area                          2018 Manual     2026 Manual
Advance Import Payments       15% of FOB      30% of FOB
PTA/BTA Disbursement          Cash-heavy       75% Electronic
Tuition Remittances           Lower limits     Up to USD 25,000/semester
Domiciliary Acct Remittances  Form A required  Form A removed
Form NXP Processing           Fees applicable  Free of charge
Documentation Violations      Lower fines      N100m + N10m/transaction
Export Proceeds Penalties     Existing terms   1% penalty for delay
Cash Declaration Threshold    USD 10,000       USD 10,000 (retained)
Outbound Travel Cash         Not specified    USD 50,000 subject to decl.

3.2 NFEM (NIGERIAN FOREIGN EXCHANGE MARKET)

- All corporate FX transactions must route through the official NFEM
  window
- Parallel market rates are ILLEGAL for corporate accounting/tax
- BDCs (Bureau de Change) can participate in NFEM under new rules
  (effective February 2026)
- FX rates are determined by market forces within NFEM framework

3.3 eCCI (ELECTRONIC CERTIFICATE OF CAPITAL IMPORTATION)

- Must be processed within 24-48 hours of capital entering Nigeria
- Required for all foreign capital inflows
- Needed for: dividend repatriation, capital repatriation, FX access
- Processed through the CBN portal

3.4 DOMICILIARY ACCOUNT OPERATIONS

- Nigerian residents can maintain domiciliary accounts in USD, GBP, EUR
- Foreign currency deposits limited to USD 10,000 per transaction
  (cash)
- Hotels can receive max USD 10,000 per guest stay in foreign currency
- Domestic transactions between Nigerian entities must be denominated
  in NAIRA
  Exceptions: Oil and Gas, Maritime, Aviation, Free Trade Zones

3.5 CORPORATE FX ACCESS

- All FX requests must go through NFEM
- Eligible categories: manufacturing inputs, raw materials, machinery,
  essential commodities
- Priority allocation: agricultural inputs, pharmaceuticals, energy
- Documentation requirements: Form M (for imports), Form NXP (for
  exports), eCCI (for capital)

SECTION 5: IMPORT DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST
========================================================================

FOR A STANDARD IMPORT INTO NIGERIA, THE FOLLOWING ARE REQUIRED:

A. PRE-SHIPMENT:
   1. Valid TIN (Tax Identification Number)
   2. Proforma Invoice from supplier
   3. SONCAP Product Certificate (for applicable products)
   4. NAFDAC registration/permit (for regulated products)
   5. Insurance Certificate (110% of CIF, Nigerian insurer)
   6. Form M (registered through bank via B'Odogwu)
   7. Bill of Lading or Airway Bill

B. ARRIVAL AT PORT:
   8. PAAR (Pre-Arrival Assessment Report) from NCS
   9. SONCAP Certificate (SC)
   10. NAFDAC Port Clearance (for regulated products)
   11. Single Goods Declaration (SGD) through NICIS II
   12. Customs duty payment
   13. Terminal delivery order

C. POST-CLEARANCE:
   14. NAFDAC post-arrival inspection (48-hour notice required)
   15. SON post-clearance inspection (if applicable)
   16. Goods can be released after all clearances

========================================================================
SECTION 6: PENALTIES AND ENFORCEMENT
========================================================================

6.1 DOCUMENTATION VIOLATIONS
    - Penalty: N100 million plus N10 million per affected transaction
    - Applies to: false documentation, missing Form M, invalid permits

6.2 EXPORT PROCEEDS NON-COMPLIANCE
    - 1% penalty on export value for late repatriation
    - Account restrictions for repeat offenders
    - Potential loss of export privileges

6.3 FORM M VIOLATIONS
    - Goods may be seized and forfeited
    - Importer may face criminal prosecution
    - Bank may lose authorization for the transaction

6.4 FX VIOLATIONS
    - Parallel market FX transactions for corporate use: illegal
    - Penalties as specified in the FX Manual
    - Potential blacklisting from FX window
