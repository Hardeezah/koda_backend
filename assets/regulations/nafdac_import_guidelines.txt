SECTION 1: ABOUT NAFDAC
========================================================================

The National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control
(NAFDAC) is the Federal Government agency responsible for regulating
and controlling the manufacture, importation, exportation, advertisement,
distribution, sale, and use of food, drugs, cosmetics, medical devices,
chemicals, detergents, and packaged water in Nigeria.

NAFDAC was established by Decree 15 of 1974 (now NAFDAC Act Cap N1
LFN 2004). The agency's mandate is to protect public health by ensuring
that only safe, efficacious, and quality products are available to
Nigerian consumers.

SECTION 2: PRODUCT CATEGORIES REQUIRING NAFDAC REGISTRATION
========================================================================

NAFDAC regulates SEVEN major product categories. No product in any of
these categories may be manufactured, imported, exported, advertised,
sold, distributed, or used in Nigeria unless registered with NAFDAC:

1. DRUGS (Human and Veterinary)
   - Pharmaceuticals (OTC and Prescription Only Medicines)
   - Vaccines, biologicals
   - Orphan drugs
   - Veterinary medicines

2. FOOD
   - Processed food products
   - Packaged water (bottled and sachet)
   - Food raw materials
   - Animal feed
   - Dietary supplements and nutraceuticals

3. CHEMICALS
   - Industrial chemicals
   - Laboratory chemicals
   - Restricted chemicals
   - Agrochemicals and pesticides

4. COSMETICS
   - All cosmetic products
   - Personal care products
   - Hair care products
   - Skin care products

5. MEDICAL DEVICES
   - In-vitro diagnostics (IVDs)
   - Surgical instruments
   - Diagnostic equipment
   - Therapeutic devices

6. VACCINES, BIOLOGICALS, AND HERBAL REMEDIES
   - Herbal medicines
   - Dietary supplements
   - Nutraceuticals
   - Vaccines

7. NARCOTICS AND CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES
   - Subject to additional import permits
   - Controlled under NDLEA coordination
   - Strict quantity and end-user controls

SECTION 3: IMPORT REGISTRATION PROCESS
========================================================================

The registration process follows a 5-STEP STRUCTURE across all
product categories:

STEP 1: DOCUMENTATION AND APPLICATION
    - Submit all required documents via NAPAMS portal (online)
    - Submit original documents at NAFDAC Liaison Office
    - Application letter addressed to the Director General
    - All documents must be complete and correctly described

STEP 2: IMPORT PERMIT AND LABEL VETTING
    - Upon satisfactory document screening
    - Product labels and artwork reviewed for compliance
    - Import permit issued for applicable products

STEP 3: LABORATORY SAMPLING AND ANALYSIS
    - Submit product samples when notified by NAFDAC
    - Samples tested in NAFDAC-accredited laboratories
    - Certificate of Analysis (CoA) must accompany samples
    - Payment proof required before analysis begins

STEP 4: PRODUCT APPROVAL MEETING
    - Food Drug Registration Committee (FDRC) reviews:
      * Dossier
      * GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) documentation
      * Laboratory results
    - Compliance directives may be issued
    - Client must respond within 7 working days

STEP 5: CERTIFICATE ISSUANCE
    - Electronic Certificate of Registration issued
    - NAFDAC Registration Number assigned
    - Product added to NAFDAC Greenbook (registered products database)
    - Certificate valid for 5 years (renewable)

Registration Timelines (from acceptance of application):

    Submission of Application:           0 days
    Document Verification:               40 working days
    Facility Inspection/Sampling:        50 working days
    Laboratory Analysis:                 60 working days

    Approval Meeting & Issuance:         22 working days

- The clock STOPS when a Compliance Directive (CD) is issued

- Delays caused by the client are not counted in the timelines

Minor Variations:
    Food Products: 60 working days
    Drug Products: 168 working days

SECTION 5: REQUIRED DOCUMENTS BY PRODUCT TYPE
========================================================================

5.1 IMPORTED DRUG PRODUCTS (Human and Veterinary)

Required Documents:
1. Dossier Screening Clearance
2. Application letter to DG (Attention: Director, DR&R Directorate,
   Isolo, Lagos)
3. Notarized Declaration (Appendix I) - notarized by Notary Public
   in Nigeria
4. Power of Attorney (if applicant doesn't own brand) OR
   Contract Manufacturing Agreement (if applicant owns brand)
   - POA: issued by manufacturer, signed by MD/GM/Chairman/President
   - Must be valid for minimum 5 years
   - Must be notarized in country of manufacture
5. Evidence of Business Incorporation (CAC Nigeria)
6. Manufacturing License / Certificate of Free Sale
   - Issued by regulatory body in country of manufacture
7. Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (COPP)
   - WHO format
   - Authenticated by Nigerian Embassy/High Commission in country
     of origin
8. Current GMP Certificate of manufacturing facility
   - Valid at time of submission
   - Authenticated by Nigerian Embassy
9. Trademark Registration with Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade
   and Investment (Class 5 for drugs)
10. Valid Superintendent Pharmacist Annual License (PCN)
11. Valid Premises Retention License
12. Letter of Invitation for GMP Inspection
    - From manufacturer abroad
    - Must state: manufacturer info, local agent info, products
      for registration

Additional for New Chemical Entities (NCEs):
- Clinical trial evidence in relevant population

Additional for Generics:
- Bioequivalence study report or Biowaiver data (CTD format)

5.2 IMPORTED FOOD PRODUCTS

Required Documents:
1. Written application on company letterhead
   (to DG, Attention: Director, Food Registration & R&R Directorate)
2. Notarized Declaration
3. Power of Attorney OR Contract Manufacturing Agreement
4. Certificate of Manufacture and Free Sale
   - Authenticated by Nigerian Embassy
5. Comprehensive Certificate of Analysis
6. Evidence of Business Incorporation (CAC)
7. Trademark Registration
8. Product Label/Artwork
9. Letter of Invitation for GMP Inspection from manufacturer abroad

5.3 IMPORTED COSMETICS

Required Documents:
1. Application letter to DG (Attention: Director, DR&R Directorate)
2. Evidence of Business Incorporation (CAC)
3. Notarized Declaration
4. Power of Attorney (valid minimum 5 years, notarized) OR
   Contract Manufacturing Agreement
5. Trademark Registration
6. Certificate of Manufacture and Free Sale
   - Authenticated by Nigerian Embassy
7. Comprehensive Certificate of Analysis
8. Product Labels/Artwork
9. Letter of Invitation for GMP Inspection

5.4 MEDICAL DEVICES

Additional Documents (beyond common requirements):
- Technical Documents including Declaration of Conformity
- Device identification information
- IMDRF (International Medical Device Regulators Forum) guidance
  compliance documentation

SECTION 6: LABELLING REQUIREMENTS
========================================================================

6.1 DRUG LABELS (Minimum Requirements)

Required Elements:
- Product Name: Brand name + generic name (conspicuous, directly
  under brand name)
- Manufacturer: Full name and location address
- NAFDAC Registration Number: Provision on label
- Batch Number: Required
- Dates: Manufacturing date + Expiry date
- Dosage: Dosage form and strength
- Administration: Indications, frequency, route, conditions (OTC)
- Dosage Regimen: Required for OTC products
- Patient Information Leaflet (PIL): Required
- Prescribing Information: For POM drugs
- Net Content: Required
- Active Ingredients: Quantitative listing per unit dose
- Warnings: Adequate warnings as appropriate
- Storage Conditions: Required
- Withdrawal Period: For all veterinary drugs
- ENGLISH ONLY: Foreign-language labels NOT accepted without
  English translation

6.2 FOOD PRODUCT LABELS

Compliance with Pre-packaged Food (Labeling) Regulations 2022:
- Name of food (brand name)
- Manufacturer address
- NAFDAC Registration Number
- Batch number, manufacturing date, expiry date
- Net content
- Complete ingredient list (in descending order of proportion)
- Adequate warnings where applicable
- English translation required if labeled in foreign language
- International Code of Marketing of Breastmilk Substitutes compliance

6.3 GENERAL LABELLING RULES

- Products with names/labels resembling already-registered products
  will be REJECTED
- Foreign language labels require English translation
- All warnings must be in English
- NAFDAC Registration Number must appear on all labels

SECTION 9: PORT CLEARANCE PROCEDURES
========================================================================

9.1 PRE-ARRIVAL REQUIREMENTS
    - 48-hour advance notice to NAFDAC before product arrival at port
    - All NAFDAC documentation must be in order
    - NAFDAC port inspection team must be notified

9.2 CLEARANCE PROCESS
    Step 1: Products arrive at port
    Step 2: NAFDAC port inspection team inspects consignment
    Step 3: Samples collected for laboratory analysis
    Step 4: Products CANNOT be sold until NAFDAC issues satisfactory
            pronouncement after lab analysis
    Step 5: If satisfactory, NAFDAC issues port clearance
    Step 6: If unsatisfactory, product may be:
            - Rejected and re-exported
            - Destroyed at importer's cost
            - Subject to compliance directive

9.3 SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS
    - Pharmaceuticals from India/China: Clean Report of Inspection
      and Analysis (CRIA) required at port clearance
    - NAFDAC must be present at port for regulated products
    - Products cannot be distributed until port clearance obtained

SECTION 10: IMPORTANT GUIDELINES AND NOTES
========================================================================

10.1 CHECK PROHIBITION LISTS FIRST
     NAFDAC will NOT process products on the Federal Government Import
     Prohibition List or NAFDAC Ceiling List.

10.2 ONE APPLICATION PER PRODUCT
     Separate forms required for each product item.

10.3 DOCUMENT AUTHENTICATION
     Free Sale Certificates, COPP, and GMP certificates must be
     authenticated by the Nigerian Embassy/High Commission in the
     country of manufacture.

     Where no Nigerian Embassy exists:
     - Any Commonwealth country embassy can authenticate
     - Any ECOWAS country embassy can authenticate

10.4 RELIANCE PROCEDURE
     If your product is already approved by a WHO-Stringent Regulatory
     Authority (SRA) for more than 6 months, an abridged assessment
     may apply. This can significantly reduce processing time.

10.5 PHARMACEUTICALS FROM INDIA/CHINA
     Clean Report of Inspection and Analysis (CRIA) is mandatory
     at port clearance for pharmaceutical products manufactured in
     India or China.

10.6 ADVERTISING PERMIT
     A separate advertising permit is required before any NAFDAC-
     regulated product can be advertised in Nigeria. Registration
     alone does not grant advertising rights.

10.7 POST-MARKETING SURVEILLANCE
     All registered products are subject to ongoing pharmacovigilance
     and post-market surveillance. Adverse reactions must be reported.
