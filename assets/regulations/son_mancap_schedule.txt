========================================================================
STANDARDS ORGANISATION OF NIGERIA (SON)
COMPLETE CERTIFICATION AND COMPLIANCE GUIDE
========================================================================

SON operates two mandatory certification programmes:

1. SONCAP (SON Conformity Assessment Programme)
   For IMPORTED products entering Nigeria

2. MANCAP (Mandatory Conformity Assessment Programme)
   For LOCALLY MANUFACTURED products in Nigeria

Both are mandated under the SON Act No. 14 of 2015.

========================================================================
SECTION 1: MANCAP - MANDATORY CONFORMITY ASSESSMENT PROGRAMME
========================================================================

1.1 WHAT IS MANCAP?

MANCAP is a product certification scheme for all locally manufactured
products in Nigeria. It ensures conformity to relevant Nigerian
Industrial Standards (NIS) before the product can be sold domestically
or exported. Established in 2006 and strengthened under SON Act 14
of 2015.

Key Features:
- Mandatory for all locally manufactured regulated products
- SON inspectors visit the manufacturing facility in Nigeria
- Certificate and MANCAP logo (with unique identification number)
  issued upon compliance
- Valid for 3 years subject to periodic surveillance
- Minimum 2 quarterly inspections per 6-month period

1.2 MANCAP CERTIFICATION PROCESS

Step 1: APPLICATION
    Submit to SON with:
    - Product details and specifications
    - Facility information
    - Production processes description
    - Quality management system documentation

Step 2: IDENTIFY RELEVANT NIS
    Match product to applicable Nigerian Industrial Standard(s)
    SON maintains a database of all NIS standards

Step 3: FACTORY INSPECTION
    SON inspects:
    - Manufacturing facility
    - Quality control systems
    - Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) compliance
    - Raw material sourcing and testing
    - Production and testing equipment

Step 4: PRODUCT TESTING
    Samples tested in SON-accredited laboratories
    On-the-spot testing may also be conducted
    Products must meet all applicable NIS requirements

Step 5: ISSUANCE
    Upon compliance:
    - MANCAP certificate issued
    - MANCAP logo with unique identification number
    - Product can carry MANCAP certification mark

Step 6: SURVEILLANCE
    Minimum 2 quarterly inspections per 6-month period
    Products subject to ongoing quality monitoring
    Non-compliance can result in certificate suspension/revocation

Step 7: RENEWAL
    Every 3 years
    Full re-certification process required
    Updated documentation and factory inspection

1.3 MANCAP REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

1. CAC Certificate of Incorporation
2. On-the-spot test report (SON lab or IPAN accredited lab)
3. Copy of MANCAP form purchase payment
4. Copy of MANCAP certification payment
5. Product technical data sheets
6. Quality management system documentation
7. Production process flow diagrams
8. Raw material sourcing and testing records
9. Product samples for testing

1.4 MANCAP FEES

Certification fees and inspection charges are published at SON
State Offices nationwide. Specific amounts are not listed on the
SON website but are available upon request at any SON office.

MANCAP e-Portal: https://mancap.son.gov.ng/
Register with FIRS-validated TIN.

SECTION 2: SONCAP - SON CONFORMITY ASSESSMENT PROGRAMME
========================================================================

2.1 WHAT IS SONCAP?

SONCAP is the pre-shipment verification and certification of conformity
to Nigerian Industrial Standards (NIS) for imported products. Operated
through third-party Independent Accredited Firms (IAFs) at the
exporting country.

Two Mandatory Documents Required:
1. Product Certificate (PC) - used to open CBN Form M
2. SONCAP Certificate (SC) - used for Nigerian Customs clearance
   and PAAR processing

2.2 THREE ROUTES OF CERTIFICATION

Route A: Unregistered/Unlicensed Exporter
    Certificate: PC1
    Validity: 6 months, single use
    Use Case: Infrequent exporters to Nigeria
    Requirements: Basic product testing

Route B: Registered Exporter
    Certificate: PC2
    Validity: 12 months, multiple use
    Use Case: Frequent exporters with Quality Management System
    Requirements: QMS documentation, product testing, factory audit
    Upgrade Path: After 4 compliant transactions under Route A

Route C: Licensed Exporter
    Certificate: PC3
    Validity: 12 months, multiple use
    Use Case: Established manufacturers with regular shipments
    Requirements: Full QMS, product testing, 2 factory verifications
    Upgrade Path: After 4 compliant transactions + 2 factory
    verifications under Route B

2.3 REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR SC APPLICATION

1. SC Application Form (completed and stamped)
2. Declaration of Conformity (signed and stamped by manufacturer/
   exporter)
3. Product Certificate (PC1, PC2, or PC3)
4. Form M (from Nigerian importer's bank)
5. Power of Attorney (in English)
6. Commercial Invoice
7. Packing List
8. Bill of Lading

2.4 SONCAP FEES

Item                                        Fee
Product Certificate PC1 (6 months)          USD 60
Product Certificate PC2 (12 months)         USD 120
Product Certificate PC3 (12 months)         USD 120
SONCAP Certificate (SC) per shipment        USD 350
Additional model per month validity         USD 10
Additional new product on existing PC       USD 350
Amendment of Certificate                    USD 100
Non-Conformity Report (NCR)                 USD 350

Note: Third-party IAFs (Cotecna, Bureau Veritas, SGS, etc.) may
charge higher fees:
- Cotecna: PC1=USD 500, PC2=USD 1,000, PC3=USD 2,000, SC=USD 350
- Bureau Veritas, SGS: Similar pricing structures

2.5 SONCAP DEFAULT PENALTY

If goods leave the exporting country without SONCAP processing:
    Penalty: 20% of CIF value OR N2,000,000, whichever is HIGHER

2.6 SONCAP EXEMPTIONS

The following are NOT subject to SONCAP:
- Food products (regulated by NAFDAC)
- Medicines (regulated by NAFDAC)
- Medical equipment and machines (not general medical items)
- Chemicals used as raw materials by bonafide manufacturers
  (for food/medicines)
- Military wares and equipment
- Contraband goods
- Used products (except automobiles)
- Project-specific products
- Products on the Import Prohibited list

2.7 PROCESSING TIME

Standard: 96 hours (4 working days) for PC and SC issuance when
all documents are submitted

Expedited: 24 hours with all qualifying documents

2.8 PREREQUISITES

FIRS Tax Identification Number (TIN) must be registered on the
national trade portal (trade.gov.ng) before any SONCAP processing.

- Paints
- Razors
- Safety belts and glasses
- Sanitary pads

SECTION 5: SONCAP IMPORT PROCESS FLOW
========================================================================

Complete import process for SON-regulated products:

Step 1: Register TIN on trade.gov.ng
    - Obtain FIRS-validated Tax Identification Number
    - Register on the national trade portal

Step 2: Obtain Product Certificate (PC1/PC2/PC3) via IAF
    - Contact an Independent Accredited Firm (Cotecna, Bureau
      Veritas, SGS, Intertek, etc.)
    - Submit product details and documentation
    - Factory audit and product testing conducted
    - PC issued upon compliance

Step 3: Apply for CBN Form M (attach PC)
    - Approach Authorized Dealer Bank
    - Submit Form M application with PC attached
    - Bank validates and processes through B'Odogwu portal

Step 4: Ship goods to Nigeria
    - Arrange shipping with approved carrier
    - Ensure all documentation accompanies shipment

Step 5: Obtain SONCAP Certificate (SC) via IAF
    - Apply to same IAF that issued the PC
    - Submit: SC application form, Declaration of Conformity,
      PC, Form M, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading
    - SC issued within 96 hours (24-hour expedited available)

Step 6: Apply for NCS PAAR (attach SC)
    - Pre-Arrival Assessment Report through Nigeria Customs
    - SC must be attached to the PAAR application

Step 7: Process NCS SGD (Single Goods Declaration)
    - Complete customs declaration through NICIS II
    - Pay applicable duties and levies

Step 8: SON Port/Border Operations clears consignment
    - SON inspection at port of entry
    - Product testing if required
    - Clearance issued upon compliance

SECTION 6: NIGERIAN INDUSTRIAL STANDARDS (NIS)
========================================================================

SON maintains Nigerian Industrial Standards for all product categories.
Key standards include:

NIS 1: General requirements for labelling of pre-packaged foods
NIS 057: Nigerian standard for bottled water
NIS 196: Standard for cement
NIS 337: Standard for steel bars
NIS 444: Standard for electrical cables
NIS 503: Standard for lubricating oils
NIS 696: Standard for roofing sheets
NIS 737: Standard for tyres

Full NIS database available at: https://son.gov.ng/standards/
